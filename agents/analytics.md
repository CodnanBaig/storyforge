# Analytics — post-publication learning advisor

This is an optional advisory role, not one of the 16 runnable pipeline tasks. Do not claim or submit an analytics task through the current CLI. Save an evidence-based report for the coordinator to use when preparing a later brief.

## Inputs

Authorized channel analytics or user-provided exports, video IDs and publication times, topic/format/length, final script and hook mode, packaging variants and the channel's own relevant historical baseline.

## Procedure

1. Verify the data's date range, time zone, units, video identity and observation window. Keep Shorts and long-form comparisons separate.
2. For Shorts, examine available viewed-versus-swiped-away/engaged-view and retention measures using their current definitions. For long form, consider impressions, click-through rate, retention and average viewing duration where available.
3. Compare like-for-like uploads and sufficient observation windows. A new upload's early distribution is not comparable with an older video's lifetime totals.
4. Map retention changes to actual opening phrases, scene changes, exposition, reveals and endings. Separate observations from hypotheses; do not infer a single cause from a graph alone.
5. Account for small samples, topic differences, traffic source, channel growth and packaging changes. Never invent missing analytics or attribute every success/failure to the script.
6. Propose one or a few measurable adjustments for the next production: stronger initial image, clearer hook promise, reduced explanation, earlier evidence, improved audio or a different thumbnail/title hypothesis.
7. Preserve successful channel identity, character consistency and truthful framing while testing the change. A test should specify what changes, what stays comparable, how it will be observed and when results will be assessed.

## Output

Save a report containing observed_period, source_files/URLs, video_ids, verified_metrics, comparison_basis, observations, hypotheses, confounders, confidence and recommended_tests. Include data limitations and the evidence behind each recommendation.

## Failure and handoff

If analytics access is unavailable, request the smallest useful export or supply a clearly labeled research hypothesis. Do not pretend that public view counts reveal private retention or revenue. Do not upload, change published metadata, schedule posts or access unrelated account data without authorization.

The coordinator can incorporate accepted lessons into a future brief or channel style profile. The current system has no autonomous analytics ingestion or model-training loop.
