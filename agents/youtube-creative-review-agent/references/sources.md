# Source basis and maintenance

Prepared 6 November 2026. The URLs below were consulted while designing the agent. They support the platform facts; the rubric weights, sample sizes, approval threshold and freshness limits are project choices.

1. **YouTube Shorts search and discovery** — https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225?co=YOUTUBE._YTVideoType%3Dshorts&hl=en
   YouTube describes personalized recommendations, choice to watch, viewing duration, percentage watched and satisfaction, plus topic interest and competition. It does not prescribe a minimum posting cadence.
2. **YouTube content analytics** — https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12220281?hl=en
   Defines the available metrics and distinguishes public counts from engaged/qualified metrics. Other channels' private analytics cannot be inferred from their public counts.
3. **YouTube performance FAQ** — https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/141805?hl=en
   Supports accurate packaging, restrained emphasis on metadata and the limits of upload-time claims.
4. **YouTube channel monetization policies** — https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en
   Requires original, substantive and satisfying content and addresses repetitive/reused output. Creative approval is not platform approval.
5. **YouTube Shorts storytelling discussion** — https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-shorts-deep-dive/
   Creator/product discussion of immediate hooks and compact stories. Its creative advice is not a numerical ranking specification or guarantee.

The agent must check current relevant primary guidance when making a platform-policy claim. It must research actual comparable videos for each new audience/angle; this reference list is not a substitute for trend evidence.

This package deliberately does not hard-code monetization entry thresholds. Those change independently of creative review and belong in the existing policy and business-planning checks.
