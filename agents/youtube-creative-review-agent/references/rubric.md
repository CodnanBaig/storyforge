# Editorial rubric

Version 1.0.0. These are project rules, not YouTube ranking thresholds. Rate each dimension 0–5, then multiply by its weight divided by five.

| Criterion | Weight | Rating 2: weak | Rating 3: workable | Rating 4: strong | Rating 5: exceptional |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| Topic demand | 15 | Comparison relevance is weak or evidence argues against the angle | Relevant demand evidence exists, with substantial limitations | Multiple relevant channels support the specific audience appeal; ordinary results and limits are considered | Convergent high-quality evidence and unusually clear audience opportunity, with counterevidence addressed |
| Hook | 15 | Generic preamble, unclear curiosity or mismatched first image | Understandable interest after some setup | A truthful, specific predicament or contradiction is immediately clear in speech and image | Economical, distinctive opening that establishes both the compelling question and essential stakes |
| Clarity | 10 | Too much background or unexplained terminology | Mostly comprehensible with one avoidable load on the viewer | Understandable on first watch; necessary context arrives at the right point | Exceptionally economical explanation of a difficult situation without distortion |
| Narrative | 10 | Repeated facts or arbitrary suspense | A coherent sequence with some slack | Each beat changes the situation or advances understanding | Precise causal progression; every beat earns its place and deepens the ending |
| Payoff | 15 | Weak reveal, unsupported conclusion or answer rushed out | The promise is answered, but impact or clarity could improve | A complete, supported ending with time to register; uncertainty is precise | A satisfying ending that resolves the promise and makes an earlier detail newly meaningful |
| Visual | 10 | Generic stills/zooms or an infeasible animation request | Clear storyboard with limited story-specific action | A coherent, feasible Remotion/Three.js plan with meaningful event-specific action | Visual explanation contributes insight the narration alone would struggle to communicate |
| Originality | 10 | Interchangeable wording, borrowed structure or cosmetic changes | Original wording and a distinct event, but familiar treatment | Independent script, interpretation and story-specific action distinct from recent episodes | A defensible, distinctive perspective and treatment with substantial original value |
| Packaging | 5 | Broad, misleading or mismatched promise | Accurate title and representative opening/thumbnail | Clear, compelling, specific promise delivered by the film | Highly economical packaging with a distinctive truthful curiosity and strong visual legibility |
| Audio/captions | 5 | Unspeakable phrasing, crowded captions or overpowering sound | Usable plan with a few readability or rhythm issues | Natural narration, sensible pronunciation notes, readable captions and supportive sound | A precise sound/caption plan that makes the reveal clearer and the pacing more intentional |
| Channel fit | 5 | Weak relevance to the intended audience | Fits the broad niche | Reinforces the channel promise and gives the audience a reason to return | Broadly understandable entry point with a clear relationship to several other valuable channel stories |

Rating 0 means the criterion is unacceptable; 1 means a severe defect. Reserve null for unavailable evidence. A missing research packet should not automatically become a demand rating of 0. A documented absence of compelling appeal can justify a low rating once research is adequate.

## Anti-inflation rules

- Give concrete evidence for each rating. “Looks good” and “high viral potential” are insufficient.
- A script mentioning suspense is not evidence that its middle progresses.
- A planned 3D scene is not automatically clearer than a simple diagram.
- High public views on a comparison do not prove why it performed or predict the candidate's views.
- A rating of 5 requires an explained distinction beyond merely meeting the checklist.
- Production expense, asset count and polished language do not earn points by themselves.

## Gate

APPROVE requires at least 85/100, hook/payoff/visual/originality each at least 4, no dimension below 3, all mandatory checks passing, sufficient current research and no unresolved major issue. Scores from 70 to 84.9 require revision. A sufficiently evidenced score below 70 rejects the draft. Missing material evidence blocks a decision. A verified fatal premise defect can reject a draft irrespective of its weighted score.

The reviewer should name at most five priority fixes before secondary polish. Every major issue needs its location, the viewer consequence and an actionable repair. Keep the line between a verified defect and a creative hypothesis explicit.
